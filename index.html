Report Runner

An internal reporting tool that connects to QuickBooks Online to generate financial and operational reports for Boulder Commercial Maintenance, Inc.

What it does

Report Runner reads accounting data from our QuickBooks Online company file through Intuit's official API and compiles it into reports used to run the business.

  • Read-only access — it does not create, edit, or delete anything in QuickBooks
  • Used internally by Boulder Commercial Maintenance staff only
  • Not offered to or installed by outside customers

Connect

Access is authorized through Intuit's secure OAuth sign-in. Only authorized company personnel can connect or disconnect the application.
