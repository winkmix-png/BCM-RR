Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Report Runner application ("Application"), operated by Boulder Commercial Maintenance, Inc. ("Company," "we," "us"), handles information accessed through QuickBooks Online. Report Runner is an internal tool used only by the Company and is not made available to outside customers.

Information We Access

When connected to QuickBooks Online through Intuit's official API, the Application reads accounting and financial data from the Company's own QuickBooks company file in order to generate reports. This may include information such as invoices, sales, expenses, payments, customers, vendors, items, and profit-and-loss figures. The Application accesses this data on a read-only basis and does not create, change, or delete any data in QuickBooks Online.

How We Use the Information

Data accessed through the Application is used solely to produce internal financial and operational reports for the Company's own business use. We do not use it for any other purpose.

How We Share the Information

We do not sell, rent, or share data accessed through the Application with any third party. The information is used only within Boulder Commercial Maintenance, Inc. Data is transmitted between QuickBooks Online and the Application over secure, encrypted connections provided by Intuit's API.

Data Storage and Retention

Any report output or data retrieved by the Application is stored only as long as needed for the Company's business purposes and is kept in systems controlled by the Company. Access is limited to authorized Company personnel.

Intuit and QuickBooks

The Application uses Intuit's QuickBooks Online API. QuickBooks and Intuit are trademarks of Intuit Inc. The Application is independent and is not endorsed by or affiliated with Intuit Inc. Intuit's handling of your data is governed by Intuit's own privacy policy.

Your Choices

Access to QuickBooks data can be revoked at any time by disconnecting the Application from within your Intuit account settings. Once disconnected, the Application can no longer retrieve new data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
